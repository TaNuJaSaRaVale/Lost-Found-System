# Privacy Policy — Campus Lost & Found

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Campus Lost & Found ("the app") helps students and staff report and recover lost items on campus. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Name, email, class/department, optional PRN/employee ID | To create your account and show who posted a report or claim |
| Lost/found reports you post (title, description, category, location, date, photo) | To show them to other campus members and find matches |
| Claims and chat messages | To let finders and owners verify and arrange a handover |
| Push notification token | To notify you about matches, claims and messages (you can turn this off in Profile → Settings) |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell your data.

## Who can see your data

- Your reports (including photos) are visible to all signed-in users of the app.
- Your profile details (email, PRN, class) are visible only to you and campus administrators.
- Claims and chats are visible only to the two people involved (and admins when investigating a report of abuse).

## Where data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication). See [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Delete my account**. This permanently removes your profile, reports, photos and claims. You can also email the address below to request deletion.
